# Receipt follow-up — the short path

The smallest complete shape Product OS supports: **four Signals → one Opportunity → one PRD.**
No Pattern, no Initiative, no Learning yet. Read this one first; it is what most bets look like.

> This example is illustrative. The product, the accounts, the tickets, and the numbers are
> synthetic. It shows the shape of a decision, not a measured result.

| File | What it is there to show |
|---|---|
| [`context/strategy.md`](context/strategy.md) | Ordered principles, so a conflict has an answer |
| [`product/signals/`](product/signals) | Four source types: two interviews, a support queue, an analytics pull |
| [`product/opportunities/`](product/opportunities) | Contradictions and coverage gaps recorded, and the human decision with its conditions |
| [`product/prds/`](product/prds) | The contract, its non-goals, and an Outcome Contract that admits what it cannot measure |

## What to look at

**No Pattern, and that is the point.** A Pattern exists when several Opportunities share one
synthesis, or when evidence must be parked before any decision. One bet with one Opportunity does
not need one — the Opportunity already carries the contradictions and the coverage gaps. Creating
one anyway is the most common way this system gets heavier than it needs to be.

**The strategy file rejects the loudest customer ask.** The most-repeated request in the evidence
is a ranking of the employees who are behind. Principle 1 — automate the chase, not the person —
outranks principle 2, so it lands in the PRD's non-goals as a decision, not as something that fell
off the list. Read [the Non-goals](product/prds/system-run-receipt-follow-up.md#scope) next to
[the strategy principles](context/strategy.md#product-principles); that pairing is the whole
argument for keeping strategy in the repository.

**The decision carries conditions, and one of them is unmet.** The `pursue` event requires three
cardholder conversations before requirements are fixed. The PRD fixes six requirements and records
an explicit, dated evidence waiver saying so. The condition was not quietly dropped, and its review
date is in the file rather than in someone's memory.

**The Outcome Contract names what it cannot do.** The company goal is stated in admin hours per
close, and no instrument for that exists. Compliance is the proxy, the baseline is one segment
rather than the flattering aggregate, and the decision rule refuses to read a compliance
improvement as hours saved.

**The loop is open on purpose.** The binding is `planned`, no measurement anchor exists, and the
analytics events the metric names have not been built. That is the honest state of a bet on the day
it is approved, and Outcome Review stays shut until it changes. For a loop that closes, read
[Best-in-class trading experience](../best-in-class-trading-experience/README.md).
