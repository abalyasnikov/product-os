## Context

The observation is the workflow, not the request. She does not lack a compliance view — she has
never opened it, and built a parallel process in a spreadsheet and Slack instead. Her own
explanation for why receipts go missing ("the mobile app is annoying") is second-hand: she has
never watched a cardholder submit one, and support reports that mobile capture completes at a high
rate. Treat her diagnosis as unverified and her workflow as observed.

Two asks in the same conversation were not equivalent, and the difference is recorded because it
changes what the evidence supports. The reminder ask was volunteered and described concretely
(day 3, day 7, then the manager). The ranking-of-people ask was volunteered twice but agreed to
quickly after the interviewer pushed, so it carries interviewer-lead risk.

Gaps: no cardholder was interviewed; the 4-6 hour figure is self-reported and never observed; this
is one account.
